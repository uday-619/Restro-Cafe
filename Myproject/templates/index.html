ABOUT US

Enjoy a unique blend of sport and socializing at our lively bar and café, set within a state-of-the-art 46-bay, floodlit driving range powered by Toptracer technology. Grab a drink, relax with friends, or take on our two outdoor 18-hole dinosaur-themed crazy golf courses. With a practice green, bunker, American Golf store, and a welcoming, cashless environment, it's the perfect spot for fun, food, and a few swings—open daily with last balls at 9pm and closing at 10pm.
